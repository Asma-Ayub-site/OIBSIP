# OIBSIP - Data Analytics Internship

## Retail Sales Exploratory Data Analysis
This project was completed as part of the Oasis Infobyte Internship Program in Data Analytics.

## Task Objective
The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset and identify useful patterns in sales, customer demographics, product categories, and transaction behaviour.

## Dataset

The analysis was performed using the Retail Sales Dataset.
The dataset contains information about:

- Transaction ID
- Date
- Customer ID
- Gender
- Age
- Product Category
- Quantity
- Price per Unit
- Total Amount

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Jupyter Notebook
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Analysis Performed

The following analyses were performed:

1. Dataset inspection and data quality checking
2. Descriptive statistics
3. Monthly and quarterly sales analysis
4. Customer demographic analysis
5. Product category analysis
6. Correlation analysis
7. Additional customer spending visualization
8. Business recommendations

## Key Findings

- Electronics generated the highest revenue among the product categories.
- Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold.
- The customer distribution by gender was relatively balanced.
- The 40-49 age group had the highest number of customers.
- Price per Unit showed a strong positive relationship with Total Amount.
- Age showed a very weak relationship with transaction value.

## Business Recommendations

### 1. Focus on Electronics Marketing
Electronics generated the highest revenue. The business can use targeted promotions, product bundles, and cross-selling strategies to increase revenue further.

### 2. Maintain Strong Clothing Inventory
Clothing recorded the highest quantity sold. Maintaining sufficient inventory and using seasonal promotions can help take advantage of strong demand.

### 3. Use Customer Segmentation
Customer spending varies across age groups. The business can use customer segmentation to create targeted offers and personalized promotions.

## Dataset Limitation
The dataset does not contain individual product names. Therefore, a Top 10 individual product analysis could not be performed. Product categories were analyzed instead using quantity sold and revenue generated.
